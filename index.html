    How it works

    Three steps between a suspicious message and knowing exactly what to do about it.

    01

    Paste what you received

    Drop in the sender's address, a full header, message text, or the phone number that reached you. No account or install needed.

    02

    AI parses the threat signals

    The engine checks domain patterns, urgency language, spoofing markers, and report history in real time.

    03

    Act on your risk score

    Get a plain-language verdict and the exact reasons behind it, so you know whether to block, verify, or ignore.
